# Awesome-Invoice-Financing-Platform

Top Invoice Financing Platforms Ecosystem

Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects
Focused on Invoice Factoring, Supply Chain Finance, Early Payment & Working Capital Optimization
Last updated: September 2026

This repository tracks notable SaaS platforms and open-source projects for Invoice Financing. These tools help suppliers unlock working capital trapped in unpaid invoices through factoring, dynamic discounting, supply chain finance programs, and blockchain-based invoice tokenization.

Examples include FundThrough, Resolve, Balance, PrimeRevenue, C2FO, Drip Capital, TreviPay, CredAble, Taulia, and MarketFinance (the category leaders).

Open-source emphasis: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom trade finance workflows, and transparent invoice tokenization — ideal for fintech builders, trade finance developers, and organizations exploring decentralized finance (DeFi) approaches to working capital.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

SaaS/Hosted Platforms

FundThrough
Invoice factoring platform that advances funds on unpaid invoices within 24 hours. Serves small and mid-sized businesses with simple, transparent pricing.

Resolve
B2B buy-now-pay-later and net terms platform embedded at checkout. Provides instant credit decisions and payment terms for business buyers.

Balance
B2B payment platform offering flexible payment terms, instant financing, and self-serve checkout for buyers and sellers.

PrimeRevenue
Supply chain finance platform connecting buyers, suppliers, and funders. Provides early payment programs and working capital optimization for large enterprises.

C2FO
Working capital platform with "Name Your Rate" technology enabling suppliers to request early payment at rates they choose. Connects buyers and suppliers in a live marketplace.

Drip Capital
Trade finance platform for cross-border SMBs. Offers post-shipment and pre-shipment financing backed by receivables and purchase orders.

TreviPay
B2B payment and invoicing platform with embedded trade credit, net terms, and invoice financing for manufacturers, distributors, and retailers.

CredAble
Supply chain finance platform serving India and emerging markets. Provides vendor financing, distributor financing, and channel finance programs.

Taulia
SAP-owned working capital platform offering supply chain finance, dynamic discounting, e-invoicing, and supplier portals. Works with financial institutions to enable early payment on delivered goods and services -

MarketFinance
UK-based business finance platform offering invoice finance, business loans, and revolving credit facilities.

Monkey Tech

Supply-Chain-Finance (reachoutlove01)
Blockchain-based platform with ERC-721 invoice tokenization, automated payment execution via smart contracts, performance-based credit scoring (on-time delivery, payment history, transaction volume), and banking integration via Plaid API. Built on Ethereum/Polygon with Node.js backend and React frontend -

StelloVault
Trade finance dApp on Stellar & Soroban enabling SMEs to tokenize real-world assets (invoices, commodities) as collateral for multi-signature escrows. Features on-chain risk scoring, flash settlements via Stellar DEX, and governance module with quadratic voting. Targets African trade finance gap of $100–120B annually -

Disclaimer

This is a community-curated list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

Invoice financing platforms handle sensitive financial data; ensure compliance with relevant trade finance regulations, AML requirements, and KYC obligations.

Blockchain-based invoice tokenization involves additional risks including smart contract vulnerabilities, regulatory uncertainty, and market liquidity concerns.

Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper security hardening, smart contract audits, and regular maintenance.
